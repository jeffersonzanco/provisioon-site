Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

Information We Collect

When you book a room at PROVISIOON, we collect your name, email address, and mobile phone number to send you your digital access key and booking confirmations.

How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information solely to:

  • Provide check-in and check-out notifications
  • Communicate important updates about your stay

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We only share data with service providers (like email and SMS delivery services) necessary to operate our hotel.

Your Rights

You can opt-out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us at: privacy@provisioon.com
